# Bloodborne for Apple Silicon 0.9.0 RC 1

**This is a release candidate.** The game now draws with Apple's Metal directly, without Vulkan or
MoltenVK: the largest change to the renderer since the port began. It ran well in our tests and
play sessions, but every Mac, area and setting can show something we did not see. Please play it
and tell us how it went before 0.9.0 final (see *Feedback* below). Online play comes in 0.9.0
final.

What's new:

- **Native Metal renderer by default:** the game draws with Metal without loading Vulkan or
  MoltenVK. On an M3 Pro at native 1080p it runs at about 38 FPS where MoltenVK runs at about 30
  in the same place.
- **MoltenVK stays as a fallback:** if anything looks wrong with Metal, choose **MoltenVK** in
  Advanced → Renderer, and please report what you saw.
- **MetalFX runs on the native frame:** faster than before (35.1 against 33.5 FPS at *Balanced* on
  an M3 Pro). The upscaler is now off by default; with Metal the only upscaler is MetalFX, and
  FSR 3.1 and FSR 4.1.1 work with the MoltenVK renderer.
- **Button prompts for the device in use:** with the keyboard and mouse the game's hints
  ("Examine", item pick-ups, tutorials) show the bound key or mouse button; with an Xbox or another
  controller, its buttons; with a PlayStation controller, the game's icons.
- **Keyboard and mouse in the in-game menu:** Q/E switch tabs, the hints follow the device you used
  last, and the mouse can click the on-screen keyboard (a character's name). Keys can be remapped
  to combinations with Shift, Ctrl or Alt.
- **Launcher:** works in small windows too (tabs at the top, Play at the bottom), and its texts no
  longer name a particular system, ahead of other platforms.

## Feedback

Tell us on the [Discord server](https://discord.gg/QS6vHz45xA) or in a
[GitHub issue](https://github.com/bloodborne-native/bloodborne-mac/issues):

- your Mac (for example M1, M2 Pro, M4 Max) and macOS version;
- the renderer (Metal or MoltenVK), output resolution and upscaler;
- what happened and where, and the log (**Log** tab → Copy) if the game stopped or looked wrong;
- how it ran: FPS in a place you know, and whether it felt smoother or rougher than 0.8.3.

**M1 and M2 owners:** one extra start helps us check that Metal answers your GPU's capabilities
correctly. In Advanced → *Extra environment variables* enter `BB_METAL_CAPS=compare`, start the
game until the title screen, quit, and send us the lines that begin with `Metal caps:` from the
log. Then clear the field again.

## Known issues

- **Online play is not in this release candidate.** It comes in 0.9.0 final.
- A crash on death in Central Yharnam on an M5 MacBook Pro is under investigation
  ([#5](https://github.com/bloodborne-native/bloodborne-mac/issues/5)).
- Blood on already killed enemies can look pale with bright edges after reloading a save.
- The camera after a cutscene sometimes starts in front of your character; move the camera to turn
  it around.

## Install or update

Download the Apple Silicon DMG and replace Bloodborne.app. Saves and settings remain in
`~/Library/Application Support/bloodborne_mac`; the launcher backs up your saves before every
start. No game files are included: supply your own Bloodborne dump with update 1.09. To go back,
install 0.8.3 again from the releases page.
