# Bloodborne for Apple Silicon 0.9.0 RC 2

**This is a release candidate.** It adds online play, a save editor and a redesigned launcher to
RC 1's native Metal renderer. Please play it and tell us how it went before 0.9.0 final (see
*Feedback* below), especially online.

What's new since RC 1:

- **Online play:** messages, bloodstains and summons through Seamless Hunt or The Hunter's Dream,
  chosen on the launcher's **Online** page. Everyone in a session must use the same pool.
- **Save editor** (Saves → **Edit character**): Blood Echoes, Insight and attributes; item amounts; adding items,
  weapons, armor and blood gems; a weapon's level and imprint, with its stats before and after;
  gems in and out of weapon slots, and the strength of their effects. The launcher backs up the
  full save before every edit. An edited save can't earn trophies and plays offline only; restore
  a backup from before the first edit to go back.
- **A new launcher look:** a calmer Home with the game's settings at a glance, blue-slate and gold
  colours, a new app icon, and **Saves** and **Trophies** in the menu (trophies can be sorted and
  filtered). The texts were rewritten, in all 20 languages.
- **In-game menus in the same colours,** and the big FPS counter also shows CPU use and the
  chip's temperature.
- **Fixed:** opening the status page in the Lecture Building, or quitting, could stop the game
  with the Metal renderer; opening the status page no longer stutters.
- **Faster game code with the translator:** the instructions it used to leave to the slower
  interpreter are now translated (in the Hunter's Dream on an M1 Max, about 67 to 71 FPS at the
  same CPU use, measured by contributor FraSharp).
- **macOS 15 or later** is now required.

## Feedback

Tell us on the [Discord server](https://discord.gg/QS6vHz45xA) or in a
[GitHub issue](https://github.com/bloodborne-native/bloodborne-mac/issues):

- **Online:** which pool you used (Seamless Hunt or The Hunter's Dream), whether you could sign in,
  and whether you saw messages and bloodstains, or summoned or were summoned.
- **Save editor:** anything the game showed differently from what you set.
- Your Mac (for example M1, M2 Pro, M4 Max) and macOS version, the renderer (Metal or MoltenVK),
  output resolution and upscaler.
- What happened and where, and the log (**Log** tab → Copy) if the game stopped or looked wrong.

## Known issues

- Blood on already killed enemies can look pale with bright edges after reloading a save.
- The camera after a cutscene sometimes starts in front of your character; move the camera to turn
  it around.

## Install or update

Download the Apple Silicon DMG and replace Bloodborne.app. Saves and settings remain in
`~/Library/Application Support/bloodborne_mac`; the launcher backs up your saves before every
start. No game files are included: supply your own Bloodborne dump with update 1.09. To go back,
install 0.8.3 again from the releases page.
